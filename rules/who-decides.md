# Who Decides

## The roles
| Role | Who | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder** | The human | Calls every play. Rules on scope, words that ship, and every question, by name. |
| **Sprint master** | Claude (claude.ai or Cowork) | Writes the plans and the handoff prompts, keeps the one master plan current, reads the builder's reports, and turns open questions into clear choices. |
| **Builder** | Claude Code | Builds only from approved plans, proves everything, reports in plain words. |

## The rules
- **Scope is the founder's alone.** Advisors, councils and AIs recommend. They never park, cut or defer an approved feature or design. A recommendation is written as a question with a yes or no.
- **Nothing is approved in advance.** Ever. A plan is approved when the founder reads it and says yes by name.
- **Rulings are numbered and recorded.** Ruling 42 means the same thing forever. Rulings go into the plan's open-questions section *before* the first build commit.
- **Blank means skip.** A handoff lists its rulings at the top with a blank for each answer. The builder skips any section whose ruling is blank.
- **Words that ship are read first.** Any copy the customer will see is read by the founder before the prompt goes to the builder, because it ships as written.
- **Waivers are named and one-time.** Breaking a standing rule needs "one-time waiver by name," with the reason, recorded as a ruling.
- **Things the founder must read go to their inbox as a file**, not buried in a long report.
- **The founder doesn't do database or ops work by hand.** Hand it to the builder or a tool, never as instructions to the founder.
