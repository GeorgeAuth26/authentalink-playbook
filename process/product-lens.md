# The Product Lens

Two models we run every feature and every funnel problem through.

## Fogg: B = MAP
Behavior happens when **Motivation**, **Ability** and a **Prompt** show up at the same moment (BJ Fogg, Stanford). When people don't do the thing, one of the three is missing. Diagnose which leg is gone before building anything.

- Low motivation: the value isn't clear, or isn't theirs.
- Low ability: too many steps, too much typing, a scary step (an ID check, an unknown app).
- No prompt: nothing reminded them at the right moment. The most common complaint in our survey of managers was "nobody told me the request was coming."

## Hooked: the 9X rule
Nir Eyal: a new product has to be about **9X better** to beat the habit of using the old one.

Our twist: the 9X rule applies at the **retention layer**, not at the front door. The first step (the wedge) needs zero inertia to enter. Make trying it effortless. Make staying worth 9X.

## How we use them
Every plan's Problem section names which leg of B=MAP it fixes. Every funnel report is read leg by leg.
