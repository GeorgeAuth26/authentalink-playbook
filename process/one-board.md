# One Board

**One list. Everything outstanding is on it, or it isn't happening.**

When work lives in five docs, three chats and somebody's head, nobody can say what's left or how long it takes. So we keep one board for the push we're on (for us, launch), and we guard what gets onto it.

## What's on the board

Every outstanding item, grouped by what it's for, each with:

| # | Item | Status | Size |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Status:** Not planned, Draft plan, Approved, Built (switch off), Live.
- **Size:** builder days, estimated by the sprint master and re-sized by the builder at each handoff. Add them up and divide by your real pace (ours is about 3 builder days a calendar week) and you have an honest date.

Plus four sections that keep it honest:

- **Order of play:** what's next, in order, with one line on why.
- **Tech leftovers:** after the push unless the founder says otherwise.
- **Waiting on the founder, not the builder:** every open call, by name. This is usually the real bottleneck.
- **Where every design lands:** each board or design names the item that builds it. Nothing is silently dropped. If a ruling parks a design, it says so by name.

Template: [templates/one-board.md](../templates/one-board.md).

## The gate: "Why now, and why today?"

Every new idea, including the founder's own, gets asked one question before it goes on the board:

> **You're delaying everything. Why now, and why today?**

Then the sprint master states the trade-off in plain words: what it costs in builder days, and what slides if it goes in. Then the founder decides, by name. A good idea still gets on. It just has to beat what it pushes back.

(Our founder asked for this question to be blunt on purpose. Make yours as blunt as you need for it to actually stop you.)

## Rules

- **The board is the list we work off.** If it's not on the board, nobody builds it.
- **Update it after every handoff report, ruling and publish.** Same doc, in place.
- **Scope is still the founder's alone.** The sprint master asks the question and shows the cost; it never drops or parks an item on its own (see [who-decides.md](../rules/who-decides.md)).
